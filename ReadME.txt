Code and data accompanying our paper on system identification of robotic fish swimming, combining particle image velocimetry (PIV), genetic-algorithm-based parameter fitting, and Gazebo/ODE simulation to uncover the hydrodynamic mechanisms behind two distinct swimming gaits of μBot, a modular robotic fish.
